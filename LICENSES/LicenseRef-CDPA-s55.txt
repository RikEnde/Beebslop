SAA5050 character shapes (bbc/src/commonMain/kotlin/beebslop/bbc/TeletextFont.kt)

The teletext character shapes in TeletextFont.kt are those of the Mullard
SAA5050 character generator, as recorded in Ben Harris's Bedstead font
(https://bjh21.me.uk/bedstead/, version 3.261). Bedstead's authors dedicated
their own work to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (see CC0-1.0.txt).

The SAA5050 shapes themselves were designed by Mullard. Bedstead's source says:

  "Copyright in the typeface will still be owned by Mullard's corporate
  successors, but under section 55 of the Copyright Designs and Patents Act
  1988 that copyright is no longer infringed by the production or use of
  articles specifically designed or adapted for producing material in that
  typeface."

Section 55 of the UK Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 applies 25 years
after the end of the year in which articles embodying a typeface were first
marketed. The SAA5050 was on sale by 1982. See
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/55.

This is the Bedstead authors' reading, not legal advice. Debian takes a
narrower view and distributes Bedstead in its non-free archive, because uses
other than making and using typefaces might still infringe copyright in the
original designs.

you hold rights in them and want them removed, please get in touch.
